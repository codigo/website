## Lead Architect and Software Engineering Consultant

May 2021 - Oct 2021

### Technologies Used

- Webpack Module Federation
- React.js
- JavaScript
- Node.js
- AWS CloudFront
- Microservices
- CI/CD

As Lead Architect and Software Engineering Consultant at MobileLive, I led the
architectural transformation for a major Fortune 500 client — designing and implementing
a micro-frontend architecture to solve deep organizational challenges around team
autonomy, parallel development, and deployment independence.

### bolsterJS

The centerpiece of the engagement was **bolsterJS** — a specialized JavaScript
abstraction layer I architected to encapsulate complex Webpack Module Federation
configuration and routing logic, making micro-frontend adoption accessible across
enterprise teams.

- Reduced micro-frontend setup complexity by **75%**, dramatically lowering the barrier
  to adoption.
- Provided a CLI tool, comprehensive documentation, and developer experience tooling
  that received **90% positive feedback** from development teams.

### Client Engagement

- Architected the full micro-frontend solution for a company that services over 50% of the Fortune 500 companies using bolsterJS
  and Webpack Module Federation, replacing a monolithic frontend that was blocking
  parallel development and slowing time-to-market.
- Defined inter-application communication patterns and established deployment strategies
  leveraging **AWS CloudFront** for independent team releases.
- Achieved a **30% reduction in codebase complexity** and a **50% improvement in
  team collaboration efficiency**.
- Decreased time-to-market for new features by **40%** due to the modular,
  independently deployable architecture.
- Collaborated with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless adoption across the
  organization.

### Key Achievements

- Reduced micro-frontend setup time by 75% through bolsterJS abstraction layer.
- Enabled a company that services 50% of the Fortune 500 companies to reduce cross-team dependencies by 80% and cut
  time-to-market by 40%.
