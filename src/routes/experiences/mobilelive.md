## Lead Architect and Software Engineering Consultant

May 2021 - Oct 2021

### Technologies Used

- Webpack Module Federation
- React.js
- JavaScript
- Node.js
- AWS CloudFront
- Microservices
- CI/CD

At MobileLive, a consulting client had a monolithic internal application shared across 4+ teams with different needs — teams couldn't work independently, and every change risked breaking other teams' features, slowing everyone down. I was brought in to prove whether a microfrontend architecture could give these teams the independence they needed.

### bolsterJS

The centerpiece of the engagement was **bolsterJS** — a JavaScript abstraction layer I architected to encapsulate complex Webpack Module Federation configuration and routing logic, so each of the 4+ teams could own their frontend independently without stepping on each other.

- Reduced micro-frontend setup complexity by **75%**, dramatically lowering the barrier
  to adoption.
- Provided a CLI tool, comprehensive documentation, and developer experience tooling
  that received **90% positive feedback** from development teams.

### Client Engagement

- Architected the full micro-frontend solution for the client using bolsterJS
  and Webpack Module Federation, replacing a monolithic frontend that was blocking
  parallel development and slowing time-to-market.
- Defined inter-application communication patterns and established deployment strategies
  leveraging **AWS CloudFront** for independent team releases.
- Achieved a **30% reduction in codebase complexity** and a **50% improvement in
  team collaboration efficiency**.
- Decreased time-to-market for new features by **40%** due to the modular,
  independently deployable architecture.
- Collaborated with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless adoption across the
  organization.

### Key Achievements

- Proof of concept succeeded — migrated a major section of the client's app to microfrontends, giving teams independence to develop and deploy separately.
- Client adopted the microfrontend approach after the engagement.
- Reduced micro-frontend setup time by 75% through bolsterJS abstraction layer.
